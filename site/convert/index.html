convert to .wfr

PLY (ascii or binary), OBJ and DXF (3DFACE/polyface surfaces, polylines, points) become one .wfr, converted entirely in this page — nothing is uploaded. Indexed meshes keep their vertex identity and attributes; DXF triangles are welded by position and keep their layer and colour.
